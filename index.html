Roadmap

ON HOLD

PF-1 "GRACE"

First flight, on hold. Joining the UK rocketry community and working toward certification so it flies properly and safely. Avionics built and bench-tested.

EARLY 2027

PF-2

Active recovery, data logging, GPS tracking. 5 km apogee.

MID 2027

PF-3

Full avionics, live telemetry, dual-deploy recovery. 10 km apogee.

LATE 2027

PF-4

Higher-power propulsion, robust airframe, thermal management. 15 km apogee.

2028

PF-5

Active stabilisation / servo fin control. 20 km apogee.

LATE 2028

PF-6

Transonic-capable airframe, aero-heating management. 30 km apogee.

2029

PF-7

High-altitude recovery and long-range tracking. 40 km apogee.

MID 2029

PF-8

Scaled propulsion, staging feasibility. 50 km apogee.

LATE 2029

PF-9

Two-stage / advanced guidance test. 60 km apogee.

EARLY 2030

PF-10

Final Pathfinder - full integration and qualification of everything Photon-1 needs. 70 km apogee.

2030

THE PROGRAMME

Three Phases to Space

30 vehicles across three generations. Each phase pushes higher, faster, and harder than the last.

01
PHASE 01

Pathfinder

10 VEHICLES / 1-70 KM

The proving ground. Ten flights testing propulsion, recovery, and avionics before committing to a space-capable vehicle.

02
PHASE 02

Photon

10 VEHICLES / 100+ KM

The mission vehicles. Photon-1 targets the Karman line. This is where the programme either works or doesn't.

03
PHASE 03

Horizon

10 VEHICLES / BEYOND

What comes after space. Pushing the limits of what one person can build and fly.
